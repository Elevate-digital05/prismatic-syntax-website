## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight, each on its own entry page:

- **International (`/`)**: owners of service businesses in the UK, Europe and North America (trades, clinics, studios, consultancies) who need a website that brings in work. They buy in USD, never see rand pricing first, and work with the studio remotely over email, video calls and a shared project board.
- **South Africa (`/south-africa`)**: South African trades and service businesses (plumbers, electricians, salons, consultants) who want a phone-first site that turns visitors into booked jobs. They buy in ZAR and often reach the studio on WhatsApp.

The job in both cases is the same: decide whether this studio can be trusted with the business's website, then take the first step (book a call or send a message).

## Product Purpose

The site is the studio's storefront. It explains what Prismatic Syntax does (websites, SEO, e-commerce, booking systems, maintenance, copywriting, video editing, brand identity), shows starting prices, and turns a visitor into a call request, an email or a WhatsApp chat. Success is an enquiry from a business that fits.

## Positioning

- **See it before you pay.** After a short call, the studio designs the homepage concept free. If the client does not want to go ahead after seeing it, they owe nothing.
- **You own everything.** Every site is hand-built, with no page builder and no proprietary CMS. At handover the files, the content and the domain belong to the client, who can take them to any developer.
- **Fast launch.** Most sites go live in about two weeks once the deposit and content have landed. Stated as "about two weeks", never as a guarantee.

## Operating Context

- First contact is a call request (Formspree form), email (info@prismaticsyntax.com) or WhatsApp. Replies within one working day. Hours are Mon–Fri 07:00–19:00 and Sat 12:00–17:00 SAST (UTC+2).
- Every project is quoted after a short call; listed prices are starting prices.
- International monthly retainer: from $300 a month, hosting included (`lib/pricing-usd.js`).
- The free homepage concept is a working homepage at a private link, usually ready one to two days after the call.
- The site takes no payments. Clients are invoiced directly and pay by EFT: 50% deposit after approving the concept, the balance before launch, retainers monthly.
- `/menu` is a printable services PDF sent by email, deliberately not in the nav and `noindex`.
- One of the founders is on every project, and the same team covers website, brand, copy and video. This is true, but it is not the lead differentiator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS with no framework, deployed on Vercel from `main`. Service pages, package cards, the client wall and the menu are generated by `build.mjs` from `lib/`.
- Prices come only from `lib/pricing.js` (ZAR) and `lib/pricing-usd.js` (USD, set by hand, never converted).
- The international homepage never mentions South Africa, rand or Paystack outside its one location FAQ and the "South Africa →" link. Service pages and blog posts are written for South African trades and link back to `/south-africa`, never to `/`.
- The CSP allows scripts only from the site itself and Whop's analytics (`t.whop.tw`), loaded only after a visitor allows it. Forms post only to Formspree. Adding any third-party host means editing `vercel.json` first.
- `check-build.mjs`, `check-prices.mjs`, `check-packages.mjs` and `check-menu-scroll.mjs` guard copy, links, prices and behaviour, and must pass before a push.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is **Prismatic Syntax**. Elevate Digitals no longer exists and must not appear on client-facing work.
- `brand/README.md` and `theme.css` are the source of truth for the logo, palette, type and material. Those rules live there, not here.
- Everything speaks in sentence case. Copy is plain and concrete: what the client gets, what it costs, what happens next.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof is never invented.** Show only real clients and real facts. A thin but true page beats a convincing fake one.
2. **Lead with the risk-free first step.** The free homepage concept is the reason to act now; every page should make it easy to find.
3. **Ownership is the promise.** Nothing in the product or the copy may suggest lock-in.
4. **Each audience meets its own context first.** The right currency, examples and contact route for the visitor's market, before anything else.
5. **Say timelines honestly.** "About two weeks" with the condition attached, not a guarantee.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Reduced motion holds movement still but never switches every transition off, because Windows reports reduced motion whenever "Animation effects" is off.
- Glass surfaces fall back to solid colour for reduced transparency, increased contrast and browsers without `backdrop-filter`.
- Interactive controls keep a visible focus ring. Content that collapses or animates stays reachable by keyboard and screen reader.
