Questions

Do you work with clients outside South Africa?
Yes. We are based in Cape Town and work with clients across the UK, Europe and North America. Everything runs over email, video calls and a shared project board. Our hours cover most of the European working day, and 13:00–19:00 SAST (UTC+2) overlaps with the morning on the US East Coast.
How much does a website cost?
The packages above are starting prices in USD. Every project is still quoted after a short call, because the cost depends on how many pages you need, what the site has to do and whether we write the content. You see your homepage concept before you pay anything, and the deposit is only due once you have approved it.
How long does it take to build a website?
Most websites launch within 2 weeks. Basic websites take 1-2 weeks, e-commerce sites 3-5 weeks, and custom enterprise projects 6-10 weeks.
Do your websites work on mobile devices?
Yes. All our websites are fully mobile-responsive and optimised for the phones, tablets and screens your customers actually use. Mobile-first design is our standard approach.
What payment methods do you accept?
We take a 50% deposit to start, with the balance due before we launch the site to your domain. The payment options available in your currency are confirmed with your quote.
Do you offer SEO services?
Yes. On-page SEO is set up properly on every site we build. For businesses competing on search, we also run ongoing SEO: keyword research, technical fixes, content and a monthly ranking report.
Do I own the website when it is finished?
Yes. Once the final payment is made, the domain, the files, and the content are yours. We hand everything over and you are free to take it to another developer at any time.
What if I don't like the design?
You see the first homepage concept before you pay anything, so you can walk away at no cost. After that, each package includes revision rounds to refine the design until it is right. If we still cannot get there, our refund policy sets out exactly where you stand.
Who hosts it, and what does it cost after launch?
Hosting is included in any monthly retainer. Without a retainer you arrange your own hosting, which for a site this size is usually a small monthly fee, plus your yearly domain renewal. We tell you the exact figures before launch.
Can I update the content myself?
Yes. We walk you through editing text, images, and prices before handover, and you get written notes to refer back to. For bigger structural changes, or if you would rather not touch it, that is what the monthly retainer covers.
What happens if you are not available later?
You own the files and the domain, so your site keeps running and any developer can pick it up. Everything is built with standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript rather than a proprietary system, specifically so you are never locked in.
What do you need from me to start, and how long does it take?
To start: your logo if you have one, a rough idea of the pages you want, and any text or photos you already use. Most sites go live within two weeks of the deposit and content landing. The main thing that slows a project down is waiting on content, so the sooner that arrives, the sooner you launch.
